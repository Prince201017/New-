Prevention

Prevention Today, Healthy Smile Tomorrow

Good oral hygiene and regular professional examinations can prevent many dental problems before they become painful or expensive.

  • ✓ Brush twice daily with fluoride toothpaste.
  • ✓ Floss daily to remove plaque between teeth.
  • ✓ Limit frequent sugary foods and drinks.
  • ✓ Replace your toothbrush every 3–4 months.
  • ✓ Schedule regular professional dental exams.
  • ✓ Never ignore persistent tooth or gum pain.
